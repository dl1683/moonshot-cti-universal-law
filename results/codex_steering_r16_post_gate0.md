## The pattern across all 13

The thirteen directions are one repeated failure cascade:

| Layer | Directions | Recurring failure |
|---|---|---|
| Assay validity | CTI, equicorrelation, benchmark bridge | The predictor was adjacent to its target, created by preprocessing, or confounded by scale. |
| Artifact consequence | AMCL, GAT | A framework, compact object, or installed geometry existed without independent capability consequence. |
| Mechanism acquisition | CSO | Once the object became genuinely causal, the learner could not discover it even in a favorable synthetic world. |
| Novelty | ACQ, CCL, PCI, VIL, CIF | The proposed law reduced to established theory, an existing field, or “give the learner more information and it improves.” |
| Ambition collapse | PCSI-1/PC-H1 | The universal claim became a bounded empirical interaction with a 3/10 standalone ceiling. |
| Tractability collapse | CR-USB/sfat-1 | The clean solo theorem wedge failed; the remaining problem is the full hard frontier rather than a selector-specific shortcut. |

That is exactly the portfolio’s recorded [four-layer synthesis](</C:/Users/devan/OneDrive/Desktop/Projects/AI Moonshots/moonshot-cti-universal-law/STATUS.md:153>).

The deeper common pattern is:

> Choose an imagined essence of intelligence → construct an assay or world where it is visible → recover or formalize it → extrapolate to a universal consequence.

When the essence was easy to recover, it was supplied by the benchmark, mathematically adjacent to the target, or causally inert. When it became strong enough to carry capability, it was difficult to acquire. When the portfolio retreated into theorem space, it collided with mature theory or inherited an established hard open problem.

The six antipatterns—beginning with the answer, replacing consequences with surrogates, putting the answer in the task, building infrastructure before establishing nontriviality, conjunction novelty, and destination-score leakage—are not peripheral process mistakes. They are the generative mechanism behind the graveyard. They are documented explicitly in the [antipattern audit](</C:/Users/devan/OneDrive/Desktop/Projects/AI Moonshots/moonshot-cti-universal-law/results/codex_steering_r12.md:91>).

There is also an uncomfortable fourteenth pattern: **thirteen directions in three days is direction churn, not thirteen independent research programs.** Several were literature kills or strategic demotions, not empirical falsifications. Three days cannot prove that no universal law of intelligence exists. It can decisively prove that rapidly generating grand abstractions from the same researcher, source corpus, and mission narrative has exhausted its expected value.
